# ⚡ EV Charging Analytical Dashboard

## 📖 Project Overview

The EV Charging Analytical Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze EV charging demand, energy consumption, revenue generation, grid utilization, and charging behavior across multiple global cities.

This dashboard transforms raw charging session data into meaningful insights through interactive visualizations, KPI monitoring, and business intelligence reporting.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze EV charging demand across different cities.
* Monitor energy consumption trends from 2018–2025.
* Identify peak and off-peak charging periods.
* Evaluate revenue generated from charging sessions.
* Understand charging behavior by hour.
* Study the impact of charging activities on grid load.
* Support data-driven decision-making for EV infrastructure planning.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### Page 1: Demand & Geographic Analysis

* Total Energy Consumed
* Average Session Duration
* Total Revenue
* Average Grid Load
* EV Charging Demand Trend (2018–2025)
* Global EV Charging Demand Map
* City-wise Demand Comparison
* Peak vs Off-Peak Charging Analysis

### Page 2: Revenue & Operational Analysis

* Grid Load vs Energy Consumption
* Charging Activity by Hour
* Revenue Trends
* Pricing Pattern by Energy Usage

---

## 📈 Key Insights

* Total Energy Consumed: 81.7K kWh
* Total Revenue Generated: 17.9K
* Average Session Duration: 158.8K Minutes
* Average Grid Load: 4.9K MW
* Peak Hours contribute approximately 63.9% of total charging demand.
* Charging demand varies significantly across different cities.
* Strong relationship observed between energy consumption and grid load.
